# Build Your Pillars

A gentle, private health tracker across four pillars: Eat, Sleep, Move and Balance (mental health). Live at https://buildyourpillars.com.

Founded by Miah Shull Olmsted and Robert Olmsted, a husband and wife who wanted a simple way to share their health journey without confusion or pressure. No insurer or provider tie, no ads, no paywall.

It began as a hackathon entry in September 2026 (Outskill C14, Group 63). Since then it has been developed by the founders.

See `CLAUDE.md` for how the app is built and `docs/development-log.md` for every product decision.
